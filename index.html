Faculty spotlight

The Faculty of Science

Two departments, a 134-credit Biology and Biotechnology degree, and teaching labs built around real equipment: PCR, gel electrophoresis, cell culture and more.

Molecular biologyPCR machines, gel electrophoresis for DNA and RNA, NanoDrop spectrophotometer.
Animal tissue cultureLaminar flow hood, centrifuge, cell incubator and inverted microscope.
Plant tissue cultureSterile culture of plant cells and tissues for propagation and biotechnology.
Microbiology and immunologyCulturing, staining and identifying microorganisms; immune assays.
