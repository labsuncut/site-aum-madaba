About

A young university with an old motto

AUM is a private, not-for-profit university founded by the Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem. Its motto, Sapientia et Scientia, comes from the Book of Wisdom; its seal carries the Madaba mosaic map of Jerusalem.

About

Milestones

  1. 2005

    Jordan's Higher Education Council grants the licence to establish the university.

  2. 2009

    Pope Benedict XVI blesses the cornerstone during his visit to Jordan.

  3. 2011

    The name American University of Madaba is approved and the first students arrive.

  4. 2013

    King Abdullah II inaugurates the university on 30 May.

  5. 2024

    NECHE grants Candidate for Accreditation status after an on-campus evaluation.

  6. 2025

    The 11th graduating class; New Hampshire reaffirms degree authorisation.

Mission and values

Wisdom and science to build a better world.

An American-style model of holistic education within a Catholic higher-education mission, open to students of every background.

  • Diversity, tolerance and justice
  • Ethical conduct
  • Transparency and good governance
Leadership

Prof. Adi Arida

President, appointed for a four-year term approved by the Higher Education Council

The seal

A map, two hands and a light.

The background is a section of the Holy City of Jerusalem from the sixth-century mosaic floor of St George's Church in Madaba. The two hands stand for the people who built the university, and the light between them for the wisdom that guides it.
