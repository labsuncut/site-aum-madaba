Sport and communities

Sport, clubs and student communities.

The sports complex has a semi-Olympic indoor pool and courts. Student communities include Ra7ala, a hiking group on the Jordan Trail, and the Mosaic Thought Leaders Forum.

The Deanship of Student Affairs runs the clubs, the sports division (with a coach and a physiotherapist), counselling and housing support.

Housing

Student housing.

Women-only dormitory

House of Grace, the women-only dormitory run by nuns: single air-conditioned rooms, Wi-Fi, laundry, dining areas and a shuttle to campus.

Opened July 2018 · 88 rooms · 3,490 m²Apply for housing
