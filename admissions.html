Admissions

How to apply

Four steps from Tawjihi results to your first class. Applications are online and admissions staff answer by phone and email during office hours.

Check your programme's minimum average

Each programme sets a minimum Tawjihi average and accepts certain streams. Use the programme finder to see yours.

Apply online

Holders of Tawjihi or an equivalent certificate, and students graduating this year, can apply for provisional admission through the online form.

Sit the placement tests

English, Arabic and computer skills. The results place you in the right level; they are not an entrance exam.

Register and pay

Choose your courses with your adviser, pay per credit hour, and pick a bus route if you commute.

Fees

Tuition is charged per credit hour.

The table shows the programmes whose fees AUM publishes. Multiply the fee by the credit hours for a rough total; registration and other fees are extra.

Scholarships

Merit scholarships by Tawjihi score.

AUM grants partial scholarships on tuition to new students by their Tawjihi average, with the highest award for 98 percent and above and a second band from 90 to 97.9 percent. Scholarships apply to tuition only, not to other fees.

Exact percentages are set each academic year; confirm the current table with the Admissions Office.

Graduate applicants

A bachelor degree with at least a "good" average from an accredited university. Sponsored employees get 15 percent off, two from the same organisation 20 percent, three or more 25 percent.

Three master programmes. Registration is 250 JD per semester in addition to the fee per credit hour. Sponsored groups from one organisation receive 15 to 25 percent off tuition.
