A. University Remedial Requirements (0 Credit Hours):

Description
Students must sit for placement tests in English, Arabic Language for Non-Native Speakers, Arabic, and Computer. Students who fail any of these tests are required to take the corresponding remedial course 0900099, 0900098, 0900097, or 0900096, respectively.
